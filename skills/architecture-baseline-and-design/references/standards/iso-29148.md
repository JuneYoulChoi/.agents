# ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 requirements profile

Authority: [ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018 public record](https://www.iso.org/standard/72089.html).

Use this profile when stakeholder, system, or software requirements and their
information items are material. Keep requirements necessary, unambiguous,
feasible, verifiable, traceable, and owned at the rigor justified by the task.

Record stakeholder needs and system/software scope; external entities and
interfaces; use cases or scenarios; functional requirements; constraints;
quality attributes and measurable QAS; assumptions; priorities; source and
owner; acceptance/verification method; and bidirectional trace links. Separate
problem/need statements from architecture solutions unless a solution is an
approved constraint.

Brownfield requirements inferred from code remain draft until an authorized
owner confirms their product meaning. For `conformance`, derive the exact
required information and process evidence from an authorized copy of the
selected edition instead of treating this summary as a checklist.
