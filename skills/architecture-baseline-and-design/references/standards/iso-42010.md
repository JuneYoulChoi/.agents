# ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010 architecture-description profile

Authority: [ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010:2022 public record](https://www.iso.org/standard/74393.html).

Use this profile to make an architecture description explainable and
reviewable. At the requested depth, identify:

- the entity of interest and architecture-description purpose;
- stakeholders and their concerns;
- viewpoints that frame each concern and the views that conform to them;
- model kinds, notations, conventions, and analyses used by each viewpoint;
- architecture decisions and rationale;
- correspondences and consistency rules among views/models;
- known inconsistencies, rationale, owners, and review triggers.

Do not assume the usual layer, deployment, module, C&C, class, or sequence view
is automatically required. Select a view because it addresses recorded
concerns. A project-specific viewpoint may reuse an established template, but
record its stakeholders, concerns, model kinds, conventions, and consistency
rules.

For `alignment`, map each produced artifact to the concepts above and record
gaps. For `conformance`, use the authorized edition to build a clause-level
applicability and evidence matrix; block the conformance claim when the source,
scope, evidence, or accountable assessor is absent.
