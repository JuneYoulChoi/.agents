# ISO/IEC/IEEE 12207 and 15288 lifecycle profile

Authorities: [ISO/IEC/IEEE 12207:2026](https://www.iso.org/standard/90219.html)
and [ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/81702.html).

Use 12207 when the software lifecycle is the primary scope. Use 15288 when the
system lifecycle and system/software boundary are material. Apply both only
when their scopes genuinely intersect.

Tailor lifecycle activities to the project's size, risk, contract, and
organization. Record selected processes/activities, purpose, entry/exit
evidence, roles and authority, produced information items, interfaces with
other processes, verification/validation, configuration/change control,
operation/maintenance/retirement effects, deviations, and approval. Tailoring
is an explicit decision, not silent omission.

For formal conformance, build the applicability and evidence matrix from the
authorized edition and identified lifecycle scope. Do not claim that following
the harness sequence alone proves standard conformance.
